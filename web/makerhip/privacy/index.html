PRIVACY POLICY

Burger Studio and 16 Track, Maker Hip's music apps for Android. Last updated: 28 November 2026.

IN SHORT

The apps collect no personal data and send nothing to Maker Hip or anyone else. They have no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and they do not use the internet at all (they do not ask for the internet permission). Everything you make stays on your device until you choose to share it.

WHAT THE APPS USE, AND WHY

PermissionWhat it is for
MicrophoneTo record your voice or an instrument into a track or a sample, only when you press record. The sound is kept on your device.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth)To connect a Bluetooth MIDI instrument, such as the M-VAVE FM-1, for notes and tempo. It is not used to find your location.
Location (Android 11 and older only)Older Android versions ask for it before any Bluetooth scan. The apps never read your location.
USBTo talk to a MIDI instrument or an audio interface on the cable (samples, songs and tempo to and from the FM-1).
Motion sensor (Burger Studio)For the full-screen keyboard's tilt and shake effects. The readings are used on the spot and kept nowhere.

YOUR FILES

  • Your sounds, patterns, songs, mixes and settings are kept in the app's own storage on your device.
  • A file leaves the app only when you choose: an export, a backup, a share, or a sample you send to your own instrument. You pick where it goes.
  • Burger Studio can hand a mix to 16 Track on the same device (the "→ MIX" INBOX). Only Maker Hip's own apps can read it.
  • Uninstalling an app deletes its storage. Keep a backup (Setup > Backup in Burger Studio, the menu in 16 Track) if you want your work later.

CHILDREN

The apps are not made for children under 13 in particular and collect no data from anyone.

CHANGES AND CONTACT

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date. Questions: write to the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
